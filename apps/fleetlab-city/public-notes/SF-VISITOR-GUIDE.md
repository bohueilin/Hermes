# Five-visitor comprehension session

Purpose: learn whether visitors can interpret FleetLab without confusing
synthetic results with operator evidence. This session has not been completed.
Device checks and AI critiques do not count as human participant observations.

Invite five people unfamiliar with the site. Ask them to think aloud, avoid
teaching answers first, and record only consented, anonymized observations in a
private study record. Do not place participant information in shared setup links.

1. Start at Welcome. What question does City Explorer help a team investigate?
2. Open Notebook. Explain the depot comparison and what stays equal between arms.
3. Explain a seed, the paired interval and the 2-point practical margin.
4. Follow a vehicle in Replay. Distinguish a completed passenger trip, empty travel,
   a queue and depot service. Explain held samples and gaps.
5. Open Source & limits. Identify what still prevents an SF recommendation and
   whether publishing the website grants permission to operate a vehicle.

For each task, record completion, misunderstanding, assistance and the visitor's
own explanation. Note navigation or readability problems separately from incorrect
interpretation. A useful outcome is a specific change to test, not a fabricated
pass percentage from five people. Keep recruitment, device coverage and unanswered
questions visible in the final study report.

For a presentation, begin with the equal-resource depot question, show the fleet
aggregate before one vehicle, and finish with the practical margin and qualification
gaps. Engineering should leave with reproducibility questions; Product with service
trade-offs; Operations with queue causes; Fleet Management with energy constraints;
Sales with explicit fare assumptions; Depot Partnerships with location/capacity choices.
