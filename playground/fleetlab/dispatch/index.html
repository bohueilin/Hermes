    An ops team would compare missed pickups and empty travel together: sending the nearest car now can leave the next rider without a feasible car.

    Best now has a boundary.

    Planned together maximizes assignments among current requests, then minimizes empty approach distance, then uses a fixed ID tie-break. It is exact for this one decision objective. It is not a full-horizon fleet optimizer.

    Read assumptions and information limits

    Integer coordinates on a 4-kilometer square; Manhattan travel at one minute per kilometer; 30-second pickup service. Decisions every 30 seconds. No pooling, traffic, charging or trained demand model. Future requests do not enter either policy until their arrival.

    Ready / idle means dispatchable. COD means executing approach, pickup service or passenger travel. VOR means unavailable for dispatch: off the drive network, not off the pavement. This fixture has no VOR activities. These are FleetLab teaching labels.

    Service rate is pickups within deadline and observation divided by admitted requests; completed trips are separate. The final population retains expired, pending, assigned and unfinished trips. No operator data or real-world safety conclusion.
