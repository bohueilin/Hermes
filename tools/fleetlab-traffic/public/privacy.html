Privacy & data handling

This local companion sends no route request on page load. When you request an estimate, it sends the origin and destination coordinates, future departure time, travel mode and chosen traffic model through the local server to Google Maps Routes API.

Google receives the request and the server’s network address, associated with the configured Google account. Its processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and applicable Maps terms. Avoid entering sensitive personal locations.

The application holds an estimate only in memory for display. It does not write provider content to disk, browser storage, simulation artifacts, telemetry or application logs. It does not cache, export, or automatically refresh estimates. Clear the estimate or close the page to remove the displayed result. Google and your network provider may maintain their own records; this app does not control those records.

The key stays in the local server environment. This companion has no user accounts or cookies. Local processes with access to your account may have access to that environment; the loopback boundary is not a defense against a compromised computer.

This notice describes the local prototype. Anyone distributing an application using Google Maps must provide their own publicly accessible, applicable Terms of Use and Privacy Policy as required by Google’s policy. This local page is not a public hosted policy.
